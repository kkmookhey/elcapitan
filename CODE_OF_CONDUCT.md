# Code of conduct

We want technical disagreement to be rigorous, specific, and safe for everyone
participating.

Expected behavior includes treating people with respect, critiquing ideas and
evidence rather than individuals, sharing relevant conflicts of interest,
protecting private and security-sensitive information, and accepting correction
without retaliation. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted
sexual attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, or sustained
disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not use a
public issue when a report contains personal or sensitive information.
Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors in
proportion to the behavior and risk. They should disclose conflicts and recuse
themselves when they cannot review a report impartially.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the
project in public or private project-related communication.
